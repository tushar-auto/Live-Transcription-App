Live Transcription

Captures another app's actual system audio, then transcribes it fully on this device.
Idle — tap Start, grant permission, then play audio in another app
Transcript will appear here after you capture and stop.
How to use: tap Start Capture, grant the system permission, switch to YouTube/Box/etc. and play audio, then come back and tap Stop & Transcribe.

Privacy: transcription runs fully on-device via a local Whisper model (downloaded once, then works offline) — no audio is uploaded anywhere.
